# The Lab — Practice Center content standards

## Product goal
Build a polished, mobile-first golf learning platform. The target is **260 genuinely distinct drills**, not 260 variations of identical advice. This is a **roadmap**, not a claim that the drills or media already exist.

## Publishing requirements for every completed drill
- Unique name, learning objective, category, difficulty, and prerequisites.
- Suitable practice locations (home, range, simulator, putting green, course), equipment, time estimate and safety notes.
- Clear setup, numbered execution steps, common mistakes and corrections.
- Quantifiable success criteria, repeatable attempt count, and optional progression/regression.
- At least one relevant, accurate instructional visual with alt text and attribution or an original diagram.
- Video demonstration where movement or timing makes it helpful; a video is not mandatory for conceptual drills.
- Verified licensing and accessibility for every third-party asset. Do not download and republish videos or photos without permission.
- Editorial and technical review before marking a drill complete.

## Status labels
- **Planned:** title and scope only.
- **Draft:** written instructions awaiting editorial review.
- **Media pending:** reviewed text, but required visuals are missing.
- **Ready for review:** instructions, media, accessibility and scoring implemented.
- **Published:** reviewed, tested, and linked in the app.

## Media goals (not yet sourced)
Aim for 300+ helpful images/diagrams and 150+ useful video demonstrations across the complete library, reusing high-quality assets when they genuinely fit multiple drills. Media quantity is not a publishing gate; accuracy and rights are.

## Data and privacy
The public repository must not contain private member records or the owner's OPERATION REBUILD curriculum. Current browser-local tracking is a prototype, not a secure account system. Avoid promises of automated swing analysis or cloud sync until implemented and tested.

## Implementation sequence
1. Finalize a structured drill data model and review statuses.
2. Expand and review drill content category by category.
3. Produce original diagrams; audit licensed or embeddable video sources.
4. Build filters for skill, difficulty, location, equipment and duration.
5. Connect logged drill results to programs and dashboard.
6. Test mobile accessibility, performance, accuracy and attribution before launch.
