Accounts and access

Is The Lab free?

Yes. Available golfer account and training features are free to use.

How do I create an account?

Visit Account, choose Create account, enter your email and a password, and follow the email confirmation instructions.

How do I reset my password?

Open Account, choose Sign in, then Forgot password? and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Why can't I open drills while signed out?

Lessons, practice drills and member training tools require a free golfer account.

Training and saved data

Are my programs saved to my account?

Not yet. Programs and some training information are saved in your current browser, not synced to your account. Using another device or clearing browser data may cause them to be unavailable or lost.

Can I use The Lab on my phone?

Yes. The Lab is designed for mobile and desktop browsers.

Where can I view my training progress?

Open the Performance page while signed in. Some analytics features remain under development and cloud synchronization is not yet available.

Still need help?
