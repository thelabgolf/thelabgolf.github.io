THE LAB GOLF / PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This notice explains what information The Lab 2.0 currently handles and the limits of its development-preview features.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information you provide

When you create a free account, you provide an email address and password for authentication. Supabase processes authentication credentials and manages account sessions, verification and password recovery. We do not ask you to enter sensitive personal information into training notes.

2. Account and technical information

Our hosting and authentication providers may process technical data necessary to deliver the website, maintain sessions, diagnose errors and protect the service. This can include device or browser information, IP addresses and service logs, subject to their own policies.

3. Locally saved training information

Some training programs, practice records and plans are stored using your browser's local storage. They are not currently backed up to your golfer account or synchronized across devices. Data may remain available to others who use the same browser profile and can be lost when browser data is cleared.

4. How information is used

Account information is used to create and maintain sign-in access, verify email addresses, support password recovery and operate the website. Local training information supports the features you use in your browser.

5. Service providers

The Lab 2.0 preview is hosted through Vercel and uses Supabase for account authentication. We do not currently offer a live contact form. Future cloud storage and support integrations will require updates to this notice before activation.

6. Storage and security

We use account authentication and provider-managed security features, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid placing confidential information in free-text training fields. Do not treat browser-local storage as a secure backup.

7. Your choices

You can sign out of your account and manage locally saved data through browser settings. Clearing local storage may permanently erase unsynchronized training records. Account deletion and data requests require an operational support process that is not yet available through this preview.

8. Children

The Lab is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians should supervise minors' use of the service.

9. Updates and questions

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Changes will be reflected by the updated date on this page. See Contact Us for current contact availability.

Development notice

This is a product-specific draft based on currently implemented features, not a substitute for legal review. Hosting logs, retention settings, provider configurations and account-deletion procedures should be verified before the full public launch.
