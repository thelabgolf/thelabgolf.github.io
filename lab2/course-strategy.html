Smart course-management habits

Play to your reliable shot

Choose a club and target based on your usual carry and dispersion, not your longest-ever shot.

Avoid the big number

If a hazard brings penalty strokes into play, a shorter tee shot or safer side of the green can be the better choice.

Track the decision

After your round, log penalties, fairways, greens, and putts in Round Tracker to see which decisions helped.
